Why an e-lab

It started with a gap between students and mentors.

Since 2023, we’ve been closing that gap one student at a time, pairing curiosity with guidance and ideas with the rigor to test them. It works. Symbianthro is the next step: a proper home for it.

So far: 10+ student researchers, 8+ interdisciplinary projects, and several peer-reviewed publications.

    About the founder

    Agampreet Saini

    Founder, Symbianthro Labs · NLP researcher

    I’m an NLP researcher working on how AI understands emotion and meaning in language. I’m a research intern with the SenticNet team at Nanyang Technological University, working under Prof. Erik Cambria on sentiment analysis and affective computing. I’m also an NLP engineering intern at Moleculyst, working on LLM reasoning and interpretability.

    Symbianthro grew out of three years of hands-on mentoring at UPES. In that time I’ve guided 10+ undergraduate researchers across 8+ interdisciplinary projects, several of which reached peer-reviewed publication.

    • B.Tech CSE (Data Science), UPES
    • Dean’s Award 2026
    • 10+ researchers mentored
    • 8+ projects
    “To build AI that understands how people feel, and to open the door to research for every student who has the curiosity but not yet the guidance.”
